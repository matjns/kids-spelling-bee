Spelling Bee for Mac
=================

This build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID. macOS will warn you the first time it opens. That warning is Gatekeeper. The app does not use the network. The warning is expected.

1. Unzip SpellingBee-mac-universal.zip.
2. Drag Spelling Bee.app into the Applications folder.
3. If macOS says the app is damaged, or that it cannot be opened, open Terminal and run:

   xattr -cr "/Applications/Spelling Bee.app"

4. In Finder, open Applications. Hold the Control key and click Spelling Bee. Choose Open. Click Open on the next dialog.
   If that dialog does not appear: open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Spelling Bee.

5. After that first Open, the app opens normally. Kids Computer can open it from the Spelling Bee tile.

Quit with Command-Q, or close the window. The app quits completely, so the launcher can come back.

The zip is a universal app. It runs on Apple silicon and on Intel Macs. It needs macOS 14 or later.
